Create a modern ecommerce website homepage for an electronics and digital products store. The design should feel clean, premium, tech-forward, and highly usable, inspired by a professional PrestaShop ecommerce template. Use a balanced layout with strong hierarchy, plenty of whitespace, and a polished commercial look. The interface must be responsive, fast-looking, and suitable for selling electronics, gadgets, computers, mobile accessories, cameras, headphones, smart devices, and home appliances.

Use this color palette throughout the design:

Dark slate: #334155

Soft gray-blue: #cbd5e1

Very light background: #f1f5f9

Bright accent blue: #38bdf8

Design direction
Make the header clean and compact with logo on the left, search bar in the center, and account/cart icons on the right. Include a horizontal navigation menu with product categories such as Phones, Laptops, Audio, Cameras, Smart Home, Accessories, Deals, and New Arrivals. Add a subtle top bar for shipping, support, or promotional messages.

Homepage layout
Include a large hero banner with a modern product image, short promotional headline, supporting text, and a clear CTA button like “Shop Now” or “Explore Deals.” Add a secondary promo strip for featured offers or discounts. Below the hero, show category tiles with simple icons or product imagery for quick browsing.

Add sections for:

Featured Products.

Best Sellers.

New Arrivals.

Deal of the Day with a countdown timer.

Brand logos.

Latest blog or tech news snippets.

Use product cards with clean borders, subtle shadows, rounded corners, hover effects, rating stars, price labels, wishlist icon, compare icon, and quick add-to-cart buttons. Keep the layout organized and visually consistent with modern ecommerce UX patterns.

Visual style
The overall UI should feel sleek, minimal, and trustworthy. Use the dark slate color for headings, navigation, and primary text. Use the light gray-blue colors for section backgrounds, cards, and borders. Use the bright blue as the main call-to-action and highlight color. Apply soft shadows, rounded corners, subtle gradients, and smooth hover states to give the interface a polished tech brand feel.

Typography and spacing
Use a modern sans-serif font with strong readability. Headlines should be bold and clear, body text should be concise and easy to scan, and spacing should be generous to create a premium feel. The design should avoid clutter and feel structured, elegant, and conversion-focused.

Functional elements
Include:

Responsive mobile-first design.

Sticky header.

Dropdown mega menu.

Product quick view.

Wishlist and compare actions.

Ajax-style add to cart behavior.

Newsletter popup.

Cookie notice.

Footer with support links, product categories, payment methods, and social icons.

Final instruction
Build the page as a modern ecommerce homepage for a tech store, using the exact palette #334155, #cbd5e1, #f1f5f9, and #38bdf8. The result should look premium, clean, and easy to shop, with a futuristic but trustworthy retail aesthetic.
