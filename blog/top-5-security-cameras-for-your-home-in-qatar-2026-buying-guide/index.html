Top 5 Security Cameras for Your Home in Qatar — 2026 Buying Guide

May 20, 2026 • AsliElectronics Team • 12 min read •
Security Cameras Buying Guide Qatar Home Security

Why Choosing the Right Camera Matters

Your security system is only as good as the cameras you install. Whether you’re securing a home, office, or construction site in Qatar, the right camera makes all the difference in image clarity, reliability, and peace of mind.

Here are our top 5 picks for security cameras available in Qatar.

1. 12MP Weatherproof Outdoor IP Camera

Best for: High-resolution outdoor surveillance

With 12MP ultra-HD resolution, smart motion detection, and ONVIF compatibility, this camera delivers crystal-clear footage day and night. Its weatherproof housing handles Qatar’s harsh summer heat and dust.

Key specs:

  • 12MP resolution
  • Weatherproof (IP66 rated)
  • Smart motion detection
  • ONVIF compatible

2. 6MP Outdoor IP PTZ Camera

Best for: Large-area coverage

Pan-tilt-zoom functionality lets you cover wide areas with a single camera. Auto-focus and weatherproof design make it ideal for parking lots, villas, and warehouses.

Key specs:

  • 6MP resolution
  • 360° pan / 90° tilt
  • Auto-focus lens
  • Weatherproof housing

3. 4G Solar-Powered Wireless IP Camera

Best for: Remote areas with no WiFi

Perfect for construction sites, farms, and remote locations. Runs on solar power with 4G connectivity — no WiFi required. Motion alerts sent directly to your phone.

Key specs:

  • Solar-powered with rechargeable battery
  • 4G LTE connectivity
  • No WiFi needed
  • Motion detection alerts

4. 5MP Indoor IP Camera with Two-Way Audio

Best for: Indoor monitoring

Compact design with auto-tracking and two-way audio. Great for offices, retail stores, and home interiors. ONVIF support ensures easy integration with existing NVR systems.

Key specs:

  • 5MP resolution
  • Two-way audio
  • Auto-tracking
  • ONVIF compatible

5. 2K WiFi Indoor Baby Monitor / PTZ Camera

Best for: Nursery or small spaces

A budget-friendly option with 2K resolution, WiFi connectivity, and PTZ control. The built-in night vision and motion tracking make it versatile for both baby monitoring and home security.
