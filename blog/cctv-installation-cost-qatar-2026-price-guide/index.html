Additional Costs to Consider

POE Switch (for systems over 8 cameras)

If your NVR does not have enough PoE ports, or your cameras are spread across multiple buildings, you need a separate PoE switch:

  • 8-port PoE+ switch: 200–500 QAR
  • 16-port PoE+ switch: 400–900 QAR
  • 24-port PoE+ switch: 700–1,500 QAR

UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply)

Qatar’s summer storms and grid fluctuations make a UPS highly recommended:

  • 600VA UPS (powers NVR + 4 cameras for 20–30 min): 200–400 QAR
  • 1000VA UPS (powers NVR + 8 cameras for 20–30 min): 350–600 QAR
  • 2000VA UPS (full system): 700–1,200 QAR

Annual Maintenance (AMC)

  • Basic AMC (clean cameras, check connections, firmware updates): 500–1,500 QAR/year
  • Comprehensive AMC (includes emergency callouts, HDD replacement): 1,000–3,000 QAR/year

MOI note: Commercial premises requiring MOI compliance often need an AMC contract with an MOI-approved company.

How to Save Money on Your CCTV System

  1. Mix resolutions — Place 4K cameras only at critical entry points. Use 2MP or 4MP for less critical areas. This saves significant storage costs.
  2. Buy a larger NVR upfront — An 8-channel NVR is only 200–400 QAR more than a 4-channel model. Future-proofing saves replacement costs.
  3. Use motion recording — Recording only on motion detection reduces storage needs by 60–70% compared to 24/7 recording.
  4. Choose the right HDD size — Calculate your actual storage needs rather than overbuying. Most villas need 1–2 TB, not 6 TB.
  5. Bundle installation with equipment — Many suppliers (including AsliElectronics) offer discounted installation when you buy the equipment from them.

Common Questions

Can I install CCTV cameras myself to save money?

Technically yes, but you risk poor camera placement, exposed cabling, and configuration issues. Professional installation (300–600 QAR for 4 cameras) is relatively inexpensive compared to the total system cost and ensures everything works correctly.

Do I need MOI approval for my CCTV system?

In Qatar, MOI approval is mandatory for commercial premises including retail shops, offices, schools, hotels, hospitals, and government buildings. Residential homes do not require MOI approval, but many homeowners choose MOI-compliant equipment for quality assurance.

How much does a wireless CCTV system cost?

Wireless cameras (WiFi) cost 150–350 QAR each — similar to wired cameras. However, “wireless” cameras still need power cables unless they are battery-powered (which require frequent recharging). For reliable surveillance, wired systems are recommended in Qatar.

What is the warranty on CCTV equipment in Qatar?

Most security cameras and NVRs come with 1–3 years warranty from the manufacturer. AsliElectronics provides warranty on all products with local after-sales support in Doha.

How long does CCTV installation take?

  • 4 cameras: 2–4 hours
  • 8 cameras: 4–6 hours
  • 16 cameras: 1–2 days
  • 24+ cameras: 2–4 days

Do CCTV cameras work during Qatar’s summer heat?

Yes, quality outdoor cameras are rated IP67 and operate at temperatures up to 50–60°C. Choose reputable brands (Hikvision, Dahua, UNV) rated for outdoor use. Cheap unbranded cameras may fail in Qatar’s summer.
