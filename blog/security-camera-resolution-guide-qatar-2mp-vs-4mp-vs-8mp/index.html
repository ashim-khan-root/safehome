Security Camera Resolution Guide Qatar — 2MP vs 4MP vs 8MP (4K): Which Do You Need?

June 14, 2026 • AsliElectronics Team • 31 min read •
Security Cameras Camera Resolution 4K CCTV Buying Guide Qatar

What Do 2MP, 4MP, and 8MP Mean?

Camera resolution is measured in megapixels (MP) — the number of million pixels in each frame. More pixels means more detail, but also larger file sizes and higher storage requirements.

ResolutionMegapixelsMax DetailBest For
1080p (2MP)2MPGoodSmall shops, indoor areas, budget setups
4MP4MPBetterVillas, offices, general surveillance
4K (8MP)8MPBestLarge areas, license plates, face recognition

2MP (1080p) — The Budget Standard

2MP cameras (1920×1080) have been the baseline for years. They provide clear footage for general monitoring and work well in controlled indoor environments.

Good for:

  • Small retail shops with limited ceiling height
  • Indoor corridors and reception areas
  • Budget-conscious installations
  • Backup camera positions where fine detail is not critical

Limitations in Qatar:

  • Cannot read a license plate beyond 5-8 meters
  • Face identification drops beyond 10 meters
  • Digital zoom reveals pixelation quickly

Price range in Qatar: 150–300 QAR per camera

4MP — The Sweet Spot for Most Homes

4MP cameras (2560×1440) offer double the pixel count of 1080p. This is the most recommended resolution for villas and offices in Qatar.

Why 4MP is the sweet spot:

  • Clear identification up to 15 meters
  • Good digital zoom capability
  • File sizes are manageable (20-40 GB/day per camera)
  • Affordable price gap vs 2MP (typically 30-50% more)

Best for:

  • Villa perimeters and entrances
  • Office common areas
  • Parking lots (single row)
  • School corridors and entry points

Price range in Qatar: 250–500 QAR per camera

8MP (4K) — Maximum Detail

8MP cameras (3840×2160) provide true 4K resolution. Every detail is captured — from faces to vehicle registration numbers.

When 4K is worth it:

  • Large open areas (warehouses, showrooms, car parks)
  • License plate recognition at gates
  • Wide-area coverage (one 4K camera can replace two 4MP cameras)
  • Forensic evidence gathering

What 4K costs you:

  • Storage: 40-80 GB/day per camera (4× more than 2MP)
  • Bandwidth: Requires better network infrastructure
  • Lighting: Performs poorly in low light if not specified correctly
  • Price: 400–900 QAR per camera

Qatar tip: For outdoor 4K cameras in Qatar, ensure the camera has wide dynamic range (WDR) to handle the harsh contrast between bright sun and shaded areas common in villa compounds.

Resolution vs Field of View — The Trade-Off

A common mistake is choosing 4K but mounting the camera to cover too wide an area, defeating the purpose.

Use this rule of thumb for face identification:

ResolutionMax Identification DistanceRecommended Coverage Width
2MP (1080p)8-10 meters5-8 meters
4MP12-15 meters8-12 meters
8MP (4K)20-25 meters12-18 meters

For wider areas, use multiple cameras rather than one ultra-wide 4K camera. A 2.8mm lens on a 4K camera covers about 100° horizontally but details at the edges will be too small to identify anyone.

Storage Comparison

Higher resolution means more storage. Here is what you need per camera for 24-hour recording:

ResolutionPer Day (24h)Per Day (Motion)1TB HDD (8 cameras, motion)
2MP (1080p)10-20 GB3-6 GB~21 days
4MP20-40 GB6-12 GB~10 days
8MP (4K)40-80 GB12-24 GB~5 days

For MOI compliance in Qatar: Commercial premises often require 30-120 days of storage. This directly impacts your resolution choice — 8 cameras at 4MP recording motion only for 30 days needs approximately 2-3 TB of storage.

Night Vision & Low Light Performance

More megapixels do not automatically mean better night vision. In fact, 2MP cameras often outperform 4K cameras in low light because each pixel is larger and captures more light.

  • 2MP: Best low-light performance per watt of IR power
  • 4MP: Good balance of day and night quality
  • 8MP (4K): Requires strong IR illumination or a dedicated IR extender for night coverage beyond 15 meters

Qatar tip: For perimeter cameras that need to work at night, prioritize a camera with good low-light specs (0.005 lux or lower) and powerful IR LEDs (30m+ range) over raw resolution.

Common Questions

Can I mix 2MP, 4MP, and 8MP cameras on the same NVR?

Yes. Modern NVRs support mixed resolutions. Place 8MP cameras at critical entry points and 2MP/4MP cameras in less critical areas. This saves storage while keeping high detail where it matters.

Does higher resolution mean better night vision?

Not always. A 2MP camera with a large sensor (1/1.8") can outperform an 8MP camera with a small sensor (1/2.8") in low light. Check the sensor size and lux rating, not just the megapixel count.

Will 4K cameras work with my existing NVR?

Check your NVR’s supported resolution. Older NVRs may only support up to 4MP or 5MP. You may need to upgrade the recorder to handle 8MP cameras.

Do I need special cables for 4K cameras?

No. Standard Cat6 cable works for all resolutions. However, 4K cameras require more bandwidth — ensure your switch and NVR support the required throughput (typically 50-80 Mbps per camera for 4K).

Is 4K worth it for a small apartment?

Generally no. In a small apartment with 2-3 cameras, 4MP provides more than enough detail at a significantly lower cost.
