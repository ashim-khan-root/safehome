## What is a PoE Switch and Why You Need One

A PoE (Power over Ethernet) switch does two things: it powers your security cameras and transmits their video data — all through a single Cat6 cable. No separate power outlets needed for each camera.

For any CCTV system with 2 or more cameras, a PoE switch is the smart choice. It simplifies installation, reduces cabling costs, and centralizes power management.

## 3 Key Factors When Choosing

### 1. Port Count

Count your cameras, then add spare ports for expansion.

| Cameras | Recommended Switch |
|---------|-------------------|
| 1-3 cameras | 4-port PoE switch |
| 4-7 cameras | 8-port PoE switch |
| 8-15 cameras | 16-port PoE switch |
| 16-23 cameras | 24-port PoE switch |

**Rule of thumb:** Always leave 1-2 spare ports for future cameras. The extra cost is minimal compared to replacing the switch later.

### 2. Power Budget

Each camera consumes a certain amount of power. The switch's power budget tells you the total power it can deliver across all ports.

**Typical camera power consumption:**

| Camera Type | Power |
|------------|-------|
| Indoor IP camera (1080p) | 4-7W |
| Outdoor IP camera (4K) | 8-12W |
| PTZ camera | 12-20W |
| Camera with heater/blower | 15-25W |

**Example calculation:** An 8-port PoE switch with a 120W budget can comfortably power 8 outdoor 4K cameras (8 × 12W = 96W, under the 120W budget).

### 3. PoE Standard

| Standard | Power per Port | Compatible With |
|----------|---------------|-----------------|
| PoE (802.3af) | 15.4W | Most indoor cameras |
| PoE+ (802.3at) | 30W | PTZ cameras, outdoor cameras with heaters |
| PoE++ (802.3bt) | 60-100W | High-power devices (not typically needed for cameras) |

**For CCTV in Qatar:** PoE+ (802.3at) is the safe choice. It handles outdoor cameras, PTZ units, and cameras with IR illuminators or heaters.

## Managed vs Unmanaged

### Unmanaged PoE Switch — Plug and Play

- No configuration needed
- Lower cost
- Best for simple camera systems

### Managed PoE Switch — Full Control

- Monitor power usage per port
- Enable/disable ports remotely
- Set VLANs to isolate camera traffic
- Prioritize critical cameras
- Best for 16+ cameras or business systems

**Our recommendation:** For home villas with under 8 cameras, an unmanaged PoE switch is perfect. For businesses, schools, or larger systems, invest in a managed switch.

## Gigabit vs Fast Ethernet

| Switch Type | Speed | Best For |
|------------|-------|----------|
| Fast Ethernet (100Mbps) | Enough for 1-2 cameras | Small basic systems |
| Gigabit (1000Mbps) | Recommended for 2+ cameras | Most installations |
| Gigabit with SFP | 10Gbps uplink | Large systems, campus setups |

**For modern 4K and 5MP cameras:** Always choose Gigabit. Multiple high-resolution cameras streaming simultaneously will saturate a 100Mbps connection.

- **Switch:** 24-port PoE+ Gigabit managed with 2 SFP uplinks
- **Power budget:** 370W+
- **Features:** Full management, fiber uplink
- **Estimated cost:** 1,200-2,000 QAR

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Under-powering** — Choosing a switch with insufficient power budget for outdoor cameras
2. **No expansion ports** — Buying exactly the number of ports you need today
3. **Mixing PoE standards** — Using an old 802.3af switch for power-hungry PTZ cameras
4. **Ignoring cable quality** — Cheap Cat6 cables cause power loss over distance
5. **Skipping surge protection** — Qatar's electrical environment benefits from surge-protected switches
