## Why Go Smart Home in Qatar?

Smart home technology is no longer a luxury — it's becoming a standard part of modern living in Doha. From controlling your lights with your phone to securing your villa with fingerprint locks, smart devices make life more convenient, energy-efficient, and secure.

With Qatar's hot climate, smart automation helps you manage air conditioning, lighting, and security from anywhere. And the best part? You can start small and expand over time.

## Smart Switches — The Foundation

Smart switches replace your regular wall switches and let you control lights and fans through an app, voice commands, or automation schedules.

### Options Available:

- **Wi-Fi Smart Switches**: Connect directly to your home Wi-Fi. No hub needed. Control from anywhere via the Tuya app.
- **Tuya-Compatible Switches**: Work with the Tuya ecosystem for voice control (Alexa, Google Home) and advanced automation.
- **Metal vs Plastic Borders**: Brushed metal panels for a premium look, or plastic borders for budget-friendly setups.

### What You Can Automate:

- Schedule lights to turn on at sunset and off at bedtime
- Control fans from your phone while away
- Dim lights for movie nights
- Create "away mode" that randomizes lights to look occupied

## Smart Door Locks — Keyless Security

Fingerprint, PIN code, RFID card, or mobile app — keyless entry is the most convenient upgrade for any home.

### Types Available:

- **Fingerprint Locks**: Quick access with a touch. Stores multiple fingerprints for family members.
- **PIN Code Locks**: Great for guests, cleaners, or maintenance staff. Create temporary codes.
- **RFID Card Locks**: Ideal for offices and commercial spaces.
- **App-Controlled Locks**: Lock and unlock from anywhere. Get notified when someone enters.

All options eliminate the need for physical keys. No more lost keys or worrying about copies.

## IR Controllers — Universal Remote for Your Home

IR controllers turn any infrared-controlled device into a smart device. Connect your AC, TV, or projector to the Tuya app and control them from your phone.

### Use Cases:

- Pre-cool your home before you arrive
- Schedule AC to turn off when you leave
- Control projectors and screens in meeting rooms
- Automate entertainment systems

## Getting Started

1. **Start with one room** — install smart switches and an IR controller
2. **Add security** — smart door lock at the main entrance
3. **Expand room by room** — build up as you go
