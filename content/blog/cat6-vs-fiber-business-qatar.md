## The Backbone of Your Business Network

Every business in Qatar relies on its network. Whether you're running a small office in West Bay, a school in Al Rayyan, or a warehouse in Messaid, the cabling you choose determines your network's speed, reliability, and scalability.

## The Options at a Glance

| Cable Type | Max Speed | Max Distance | Cost per Meter | Best For |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|----------------|----------|
| Cat6 | 10 Gbps (55m) / 1 Gbps (100m) | 100m | Low | Offices, CCTV, general use |
| Cat6a | 10 Gbps (100m) | 100m | Medium | Data-heavy offices, future-proofing |
| Fiber (Single-mode) | 100+ Gbps | 40km+ | High | Campus networks, long runs, data centers |
| Fiber (Multi-mode) | 10-40 Gbps | 300-400m | Medium-High | Building-to-building, high-speed backbones |

## When to Choose Cat6

**Cat6 is the right choice when:**
- Your office is under 100m per cable run
- You need 1 Gbps to the desk (sufficient for 95% of users)
- You're powering PoE devices (cameras, access points, phones)
- Budget is a primary concern

**Best for Qatar businesses:** Small to medium offices, retail shops, schools, and CCTV installations.

Cat6 supports PoE and PoE+ (Power over Ethernet), meaning security cameras, wireless access points, and VoIP phones can all be powered through the same cable — no separate electrical wiring needed.

**Key spec:** Our Cat6 cables come in CCA (Copper-Clad Aluminum) and pure bare copper. For critical installations, always choose bare copper for better signal integrity.

### Cable Quality Matters

In Qatar's climate, cable quality directly impacts network reliability:

- **Bare copper**: Best conductivity, less signal loss over distance
- **CCA (Copper-Clad Aluminum)**: Budget-friendly, fine for short runs
- **Shielding (STP/FTP)**: Reduces interference in areas with electrical noise

## When to Choose Fiber Optic

**Fiber optic is the right choice when:**
- Runs exceed 100 meters
- You need 10 Gbps or higher speeds
- Connecting multiple buildings on a campus
- Future-proofing a new installation
- Electrical interference is a concern

**Best for Qatar businesses:** Large office buildings, education campuses, industrial facilities, data centers.

Fiber is immune to electromagnetic interference, making it ideal for industrial environments and areas with heavy electrical equipment.

## The Hybrid Approach

Many businesses in Qatar benefit from a hybrid setup:

```
Fiber backbone (building-to-building)
  └── Cat6 to the desk (last 100m)
      └── PoE for cameras and access points
```

This gives you fiber's speed and distance for the backbone, with Cat6's affordability and PoE capability at the endpoint.

## Installation Considerations in Qatar

### Professional Installation

Cabling is not a DIY job for critical business networks. Professional installation ensures:

- **Proper termination** — loose connectors cause intermittent issues
- **Cable testing** — each run tested for continuity, speed, and interference
- **Cable management** — organized racks and labelled patch panels
- **Compliance** — adherence to local building and safety standards

### Structured Cabling

A structured cabling approach means:

- All cables terminate at a central patch panel
- Organized cable management for easy maintenance
- Labelled every connection for troubleshooting
- Expansion capacity built in

## Making the Right Choice

**For most businesses in Qatar:** Cat6 (bare copper) is the practical choice. It handles current needs well, supports PoE, and is cost-effective.

**For new builds and expansions:** Run fiber between buildings and Cat6a to the desk. The extra cost for Cat6a (vs Cat6) is minimal when you're already running new cables, and it gives you 10 Gbps up to 100 meters.
