## What Is a Solar 4G Security Camera?

A solar 4G security camera runs on renewable solar energy and connects through the 4G mobile network instead of WiFi. It needs no electrical wiring, no internet connection, and no monthly electricity cost. The built-in solar panel charges a rechargeable battery that keeps the camera running 24/7 — even during Qatar's cloudy winter days and during power outages.

## Why Solar + 4G Instead of WiFi in Qatar?

Most security cameras assume you have reliable WiFi and easy access to power. Qatar's properties present three problems these cameras cannot solve:

- **Villas and compounds** have large outdoor areas far from the router. WiFi signal drops before you reach the gate.
- **Construction sites** have no WiFi and no power installed yet. Security is needed from day one.
- **Remote properties** like farms, warehouses, and labour camps have neither internet nor electrical infrastructure.

A solar 4G camera bypasses all three problems. Insert a local SIM card — Ooredoo, Vodafone, or STC — and the camera connects to the 4G network directly. Installation takes 10 minutes with no electrician.

## Is a Solar 4G Camera Reliable in Qatar's Climate?

Qatar averages over 300 sunny days per year. A high-efficiency solar panel generates enough charge in 3-4 hours of direct sunlight to power the camera for 24-48 hours continuously. The internal battery stores 3-5 days of reserve power, so consecutive cloudy days or dust storms won't interrupt operation.

The camera housing is IP66-rated — completely protected against dust ingress and powerful water jets. It operates reliably in temperatures from -20°C to 60°C. The solar panel itself is tempered glass rated for extreme UV exposure and sand abrasion.

## How to Install a Solar 4G Security Camera (5 Minutes)

1. Mount the bracket on a wall or pole at 2.5-3 metres height for the best field of view while remaining accessible for panel cleaning.
2. Position the solar panel to face south or southwest for maximum sunlight exposure throughout the day.
3. Insert a standard 4G SIM card from Ooredoo, Vodafone Qatar, or STC. A 10-20 GB prepaid data plan per camera per month is sufficient.
4. Download the Secuview app from iOS or Android. Scan the QR code on the camera body to pair.
5. Adjust the camera angle and motion detection zone through the app.

No electrician. No technician. No WiFi password. No cable burial. Ten minutes, from box to live feed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does a solar 4G camera work without WiFi?**
Yes. The camera connects directly to the 4G mobile network through a standard SIM card. No WiFi, no router, no broadband internet subscription is required.

**How long does the battery last on cloudy days?**
The internal battery stores enough power for 3-5 days of normal operation without any sunlight. Qatar averages 300+ sunny days per year, so extended battery-only operation is rare.

**Can I install it myself?**
Yes. The camera is completely wire-free. Mount the bracket, insert a SIM card, download the app, and scan the QR code. No electrician required.

**What SIM card works in Qatar?**
Any standard 4G SIM card from Ooredoo, Vodafone Qatar, or STC works. A prepaid data SIM with 10-20 GB per month per camera is sufficient.

**Does the camera record continuously or only on motion?**
It records when PIR motion detection is triggered. You can also configure scheduled recording in the app. Continuous recording is not recommended for battery conservation.

**Does the solar panel work in Qatar's summer heat?**
Yes. The solar panel and battery are rated for temperatures up to 60°C. The IP66 weatherproof housing protects against dust, direct sunlight, and rain.

**What happens if someone steals the camera?**
The camera records to a microSD card (up to 128 GB). If the camera is disconnected, the last recorded footage remains on the card. The mounting bracket stays on the wall.

**Can multiple people view the same camera?**
Yes. The app supports multi-user access. Family members or security staff can log in from their own devices simultaneously.
