Planning a CCTV security system requires precise calculations — storage capacity, PoE power budgets, network bandwidth, UPS runtime, and camera field of view. Get these wrong and you risk recording gaps, switch overloads, or cameras going offline.

At [FreeToolz.in](https://freetoolz.in/), there are free browser-based calculators for all these planning tasks. No signup, no data collection — just instant results.

## CCTV Storage Calculator

Storage is the most common mistake in CCTV planning. An 8-camera system at 4MP resolution recording 24/7 can fill a 4TB drive in under two weeks.

Use the [CCTV Storage Calculator](https://freetoolz.in/tools/cctv-storage-calculator/) to factor in:
- Number of cameras and resolution
- Frames per second and compression (H.264 vs H.265)
- Recording mode (continuous vs motion-triggered)
- Required retention period

For most villa installations in Qatar, we recommend a minimum of 14 days of storage. For commercial properties, 30 days is standard — and MOI-regulated sites require 120 days.

## PoE Calculator

PoE switch selection is about matching total camera power draw to the switch budget. Exceed it and cameras will intermittently reboot.

The [PoE Calculator](https://freetoolz.in/tools/poe-calculator/) helps you:
- Add cameras with different PoE classes (802.3af, 802.3at PoE+)
- Calculate total power draw
- Determine the right switch capacity with 20% headroom

For example, 8 cameras at 12W each need a switch with at least 115W PoE budget. A 120W switch leaves almost no room for expansion — a 200W switch is safer.

Check our selection of [PoE switches](https://aslielectronics.com/categories/network--communications/) for your installation.

## Bandwidth Calculator

IP cameras can saturate a network. Each 4MP camera at 20 FPS uses roughly 8 Mbps. For 16 cameras, that's 128 Mbps — exceeding most consumer routers.

The [Bandwidth Calculator](https://freetoolz.in/tools/bandwidth-calculator/) estimates total network load based on your camera specs, helping you decide if you need managed switches and VLAN segmentation.

## Camera FOV Calculator

Field of view determines how much area a camera covers. A 2.8mm lens gives wide coverage ~100°, while 6mm zooms in to ~45°.

The [Camera FOV Calculator](https://freetoolz.in/tools/fov-calculator/) is essential for planning camera placement before installation.

## UPS Runtime Calculator

A power outage during a security event defeats the purpose of your system. The [UPS Runtime Calculator](https://freetoolz.in/tools/ups-calculator/) sizes your backup power based on total system load and required runtime.

A typical Qatar villa setup — NVR (50W) + 8 cameras via PoE switch (80W) — needs roughly 130W. A 650VA UPS gives about 45 minutes of runtime.

## RAID Storage Calculator

For commercial installations, RAID protects against drive failure data loss. The [RAID Storage Calculator](https://freetoolz.in/tools/raid-calculator/) computes usable capacity across RAID levels.

MOI-compliant installations in Qatar typically require RAID 5 or RAID 6 for redundancy plus adequate capacity.
